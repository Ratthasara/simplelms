# Lecturer - Read Me

## Purpose
Lecturers use the LMS to manage their assigned subjects, enroll students, deliver materials, create assignments, take attendance, and review results.

## Main Navigation
- `Dashboard`: opens your Workspace and current subject cards.
- `My Subjects`: opens the full list of your active subjects.
- `ID`: view and print your LMS ID card.
- `Log Out`: exit the portal.

Depending on your institutional setup, and whether Simple Editors is active, you may not see `Editor Tools` or `Academic Management`.

## What You Can Do
- Access only the subjects assigned to you.
- Enroll students into your subjects.
- Add subject materials.
- Create assignments with deadlines and weights.
- Start discussion topics and respond to student threads.
- Take attendance using the portrait-based attendance board.
- Review grades, progress, and attendance summaries.

## My Subjects
Open `My Subjects` to see your active subject list. Click any subject to open its full page workspace.

## Subject Workspace
Each subject includes these tabs:
- `Materials`
- `Assignments`
- `Discussions`
- `Attendance`
- `People`
- `Results`

Materials and assignments show the latest numbered week first and keep General items at the bottom. Your `Order In Week` values remain authoritative within each week; unordered items use newest-first publication order. Discussion topics show the newest thread first, while replies remain oldest-to-newest inside each thread.

### Materials
Add subject materials with:
- title
- description
- file upload
- up to 10 video or audio embed links
- media such as text, image, audio, or video links

### Assignments
Create assignments with:
- title
- instructions
- supporting files
- up to 10 video or audio embed links
- deadline
- weight
- student submission type

New assignments default to `Typed answer or file upload`, so students can type an answer, upload a file, or provide both unless you choose a stricter option.

### Late submission policy
The late policy is shown to students at the end of each assignment submission form. Students may submit or update late work until the submission has been graded. The lecturer's raw score is preserved, but the final assignment contribution is reduced by 5% for each started day after the deadline, up to 10 late days. Submissions more than 10 days late can still be graded but contribute 0.00 toward the final result.

Late status and penalties are recalculated from the latest submission time and the assignment's current deadline. Extending a deadline can remove a late status and penalty; shortening it can add them, including for graded work. All LMS deadlines and submission times use Yangon time (UTC/GMT +6:30), independently of the WordPress site timezone.

### Discussions
Use discussions for class conversation.
- A welcome thread is created when a subject is published.
- You and students can start new threads.
- New topics can include up to 10 video or audio embed links.
- Replies can include attachments.

### Attendance
Attendance uses a visual board.
1. Open `Attendance`.
2. Set or confirm total classes for the subject.
3. Open a class such as `Week 1`, `Week 2`, and so on.
4. Students appear as portrait tiles.
5. They are absent by default.
6. Click a student portrait to mark present.
7. Save the class attendance.

Green indicates present. Red indicates absent.

### People
Use `People` to review the current roster and confirm that the correct students are enrolled.

### Results
Use `Results` to review assignment performance, attendance contribution, and overall progress.

## Common Lecturer Tasks
### Enroll students
1. Open the subject.
2. Go to the enrollment or people workflow for that subject.
3. Add student codes manually or import from a file if available.

### Prepare a subject before teaching starts
1. Confirm subject information and total classes.
2. Add core materials.
3. Create assignments and weights.
4. Review the welcome discussion thread.
5. Confirm the student roster.

## Good Practice
- Set total classes before the first attendance session.
- Add materials and assignments in a structured order.
- Use clear deadlines and assignment titles.
- Review attendance and results weekly.

## Caution
- You only have authority over assigned subjects.
- Confirm the correct subject before editing grades or attendance.
