# Admin - Read Me

## Email Center
Open **Email Center** from the LMS dashboard to send email-only announcements to all active students, all staff, or selected class groups. Email Center messages do not appear as broadcasts or portal notices.

Review the subject, message, and recipient groups, send a test to your administrator address when needed, confirm the campaign, and queue it for asynchronous delivery. Campaign history shows queued, sent, failed, cancelled, and skipped totals. Pending deliveries can be cancelled, and failed deliveries can be retried.

## Purpose
Admins have full institutional control across the LMS. Use this role for platform ownership, academic setup, high-level records oversight, and permission-sensitive changes.

## Main Navigation
- `Dashboard`: opens the Workspace overview with your current role, subject totals, and direct entry cards.
- `Editor Tools`: manage institutional publishing items from the frontend when the Simple Editors add-on is active.
- `Academic Management`: manage programs, terms, subjects, people, and imports.
- `My Subjects`: open subject workspaces you are assigned to or enrolled in.
- `ID`: view and print your LMS ID card.
- `Log Out`: exit the portal.

## What You Can Do
- Create and manage programs.
- Create, generate, and update terms.
- Create, edit, duplicate, publish, and archive subjects.
- Assign lecturers to subjects.
- Add students and staff manually.
- Import students, staff, and subjects from CSV or XLSX templates.
- Enroll students in subjects.
- Access Editor Tools for news, announcements, journal articles, thesis entries, and admission status when Simple Editors is active.
- View and manage attendance, grades, discussions, assignments, and records across all subjects.
- View completed-course records and transcripts for students.

## Academic Management
Use `Academic Management` for:
- `Programs`: create and maintain program structures.
- `Terms`: create new terms or use the automated academic-year generation workflow.
- `Subjects`: add new subjects, assign lecturers, set total classes, and duplicate subjects for future terms.
- `People`: add students and staff, update contact details, and review recent profiles.
- `Imports`: upload CSV/XLSX files for students, staff, and subjects.

## Editor Tools
Use the Simple Editors add-on `Editor Tools` for:
- `News`: publish new posts and manage existing posts from the frontend.
- `Announcements`: publish and edit announcement posts from the frontend.
- `Journal Articles`: publish and manage journal records from the frontend.
- `Thesis Entries`: add and edit thesis records from the frontend.
- `Admission Status`: update public admission messaging.

## Subject Workspace
Open a subject from `My Subjects` or from a Workspace card.

Inside a subject:
- `Materials`: upload or add teaching resources.
- `Assignments`: create assignments, deadlines, and weights.
- `Discussions`: moderate or participate in discussion threads.
- `Attendance`: define class count and review or edit attendance records.
- `People`: view enrolled students and staff linked to the subject.
- `Results`: review gradebook results and student progress.

## Common Admin Tasks
### Set up a new academic year
1. Open `Academic Management`.
2. Create or generate next-year terms.
3. Add or duplicate subjects into the target term.
4. Assign lecturers.
5. Import or add students and staff.
6. Enroll students into subjects.

### Correct a student record
1. Open the subject workspace.
2. Go to `Attendance` or `Results`.
3. Update the relevant attendance session or grading item.
4. Save changes and confirm the student view reflects the update.

## Good Practice
- Use imports for bulk onboarding and manual add forms for exceptions.
- Keep lecturer assignment accurate before subjects go live.
- Review total classes for each subject before attendance begins.
- Use the frontend management screens rather than mixing data changes between frontend and wp-admin.

## Caution
- Admin actions affect live academic records.
- Before large imports or mass edits, back up the site database.
- Confirm subject term, lecturer assignment, and total classes before publishing.
