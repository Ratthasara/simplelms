# Student - Read Me

## Purpose
Students use the LMS to access enrolled subjects, view materials, submit assignments, join discussions, check attendance, and review completed-course records.

## Main Navigation
- `Dashboard`: opens your Workspace with current subject cards and personal tools.
- `My Subjects`: shows all active subjects currently available to you.
- `ID`: view, print, and update your LMS ID card and profile photo.
- `Log Out`: exit the portal.

If available to your account, you may also see a transcript route from the Workspace cards.

## What You Can Do
- Access only subjects you are enrolled in.
- Open materials, assignments, discussions, attendance, and results within each subject.
- Submit assignments.
- Participate in discussions.
- Track absences and progress.
- View completed courses and print a formal transcript.

## Access Window
Subjects remain available for 12 months from your enrollment date. After that, they no longer appear in your active subject list.

## My Subjects
Open `My Subjects` to see your current subject list. Click a subject to open its full workspace page.

## Subject Workspace
Each subject includes:
- `Materials`: lecture notes, files, links, and media
- `Assignments`: current tasks, deadlines, and submission areas
- `Discussions`: threads and replies with your class
- `Attendance`: your attendance progress and absences
- `People`: class roster view if enabled
- `Results`: assignment scores, attendance contribution, and overall progress

Materials and assignments show the latest numbered week first, with General items at the bottom. Discussion topics show the newest thread first, while replies inside a thread remain in conversational order from oldest to newest.

## ID Card
Open `ID` to:
- view your digital ID
- print it
- upload or replace your photo
- crop the image before saving

Photo upload limit is 10 MB.

## Transcript
When you have completed subjects, your transcript becomes available from the Workspace.

The transcript includes:
- institution branding
- your student information
- completed subjects
- grades
- credits
- CGPA summary

Use `Print / Save PDF` to keep a digital copy.

## Common Student Tasks
### Submit an assignment
1. Open `My Subjects`.
2. Open the subject.
3. Go to `Assignments`.
4. Open the relevant assignment.
5. Type your answer, upload a file, or provide both when the assignment allows it.
6. Use the `Submit Assignment` button to send your work.

### Late submission policy
The late policy is shown at the end of each assignment submission form. Late work can be submitted or updated until it has been graded. Your lecturer's raw score is preserved, but the final assignment contribution is reduced by 5% for each started day after the deadline, up to 10 late days. Submissions more than 10 days late can still be graded but contribute 0.00 toward the final result.

Late status and penalties always use the assignment's current deadline. If a lecturer extends or shortens the deadline after you submit, the displayed status and calculated contribution update automatically. All LMS deadlines and submission times use Yangon time (UTC/GMT +6:30).

### Check your attendance
1. Open the subject.
2. Go to `Attendance`.
3. Review recorded classes, absence count, and attendance percentage.

### Join a discussion
1. Open the subject.
2. Go to `Discussions`.
3. Open a thread or create a new one.
4. Add your text and attachments if needed.

## Good Practice
- Check subject pages regularly for new materials and deadlines.
- Review attendance often so you can catch missing records early.
- Keep your profile photo current for your LMS ID.

## Caution
- Subject access expires after the active access window ends.
- If a grade or attendance record looks wrong, contact your lecturer or the academic office promptly.
