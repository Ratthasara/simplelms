# Officer - Read Me

## Purpose
Officers handle day-to-day academic operations. This role is intended for registrar-style workflows, onboarding, records maintenance, and institutional coordination.

## Main Navigation
- `Dashboard`: opens your Workspace with operational cards and current totals.
- `Editor Tools`: available for institutional publishing tasks when the Simple Editors add-on is active.
- `Academic Management`: your main area for terms, subjects, people, and imports.
- `My Subjects`: opens subjects you are directly linked to.
- `ID`: view and print your LMS ID card.
- `Log Out`: exit the portal.

## What You Can Do
- Add and manage programs, terms, and subjects.
- Duplicate subjects into future terms.
- Assign lecturers to subjects.
- Add students and staff manually.
- Import students, staff, and subjects from spreadsheet templates.
- Enroll students into subjects.
- View and edit attendance and grades across subjects.
- Access Editor Tools for announcements and institutional publishing when Simple Editors is active.
- View records needed for academic operations and student support.

## Academic Management
This is your main operational area.

Use it to:
- generate or create terms
- maintain subject lists
- set subject total classes for attendance
- assign lecturers
- add or import people
- manage student enrollment

## Managing Students and Staff
For quick onboarding:
1. Open `Academic Management`.
2. Use the quick-add forms for one-off additions.
3. Use the import templates for bulk additions.
4. Confirm person code, email, program, and intake term where relevant.

## Records Oversight
Officers can review:
- subject attendance history
- current and completed enrollments
- assignment and grade progress
- completed-course transcript data for students

Use subject pages when you need course-level detail, and use Academic Management for structural changes.

## Subject Workspace
Inside each subject:
- `Materials`: review posted learning resources.
- `Assignments`: review current assessment setup.
- `Discussions`: review subject activity.
- `Attendance`: inspect or correct attendance data.
- `People`: verify the roster.
- `Results`: inspect grades and progress.

## Common Officer Tasks
### Start a term
1. Generate or create the term.
2. Duplicate or create subjects for that term.
3. Confirm lecturer assignment.
4. Import student lists or add students manually.
5. Enroll students into the correct subjects.

### Support a lecturer
1. Open the relevant subject.
2. Check enrollment, attendance class count, and assignment setup.
3. Edit attendance or grades if authorized by institutional process.

## Good Practice
- Validate import files against the bundled templates before upload.
- Keep subject metadata clean: code, title, term, credits, lecturers, total classes.
- Use subject duplication for repeat offerings to reduce setup errors.

## Caution
- Officers can change operational records. Check the correct term and subject before editing.
- Do not use Officer access for system-level plugin or capability changes. Those remain admin work.
